Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Mega Power AI (Android app com.umeshchaudhary.megapowerai and the web app served from this repository) is built with privacy as the default. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

1. The short version

  • We do not operate any server that receives your data.
  • Your chats, projects, image history, settings, API keys and account data are stored only on your device.
  • When you use an AI feature, your prompt is sent directly from your device to the AI provider that powers that feature (section 3).
  • No analytics. No ads. No tracking. No data collection by the developer.

2. Data stored on your device

DataWherePurpose
Chats & conversationsDevice storageYour chat history
Projects & filesDevice storageYour saved projects
Image/video historyDevice storageYour generated media list
Settings & themeDevice storageYour preferences
API keys & GitHub token (optional)Device storageSent only to the provider they belong to
Local account (optional)Device storageLogin convenience — passwords are hashed

You can export or delete all of this anytime in Settings → Data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Third-party AI services (only when you use them)

  • Chat, code generation, auto-fix: sent directly to the AI engine you select — by default a free keyless engine (Pollinations.ai), or your own API-key provider (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral). These providers process your prompt under their own privacy policies.
  • Image generation: your image prompt is sent to the free image engine (Pollinations.ai).
  • Voice narration: uses your device's built-in speech engine — no network needed.
  • Video generation: happens entirely on your device.
  • GitHub features (only if you connect a token): your token and API calls go directly from your device to api.github.com.

The Internet permission is used solely for these AI/API requests.

4. Children

The app is a general productivity tool and is not directed at children under 13.

5. Your rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export — everything is already yours, on your own device, fully exportable and deletable in Settings.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
