# Privacy Policy — Mega Power AI

**Last updated: September 2026**

Mega Power AI ("the app", Android package `com.umeshchaudhary.megapowerai`, and the web app served from this repository) is built with privacy as the default. This policy explains exactly what happens to your data.

## 1. The short version

- We do **not** operate any server that receives your data.
- Your chats, projects, images history, settings, API keys and account data are stored **only on your device** (browser localStorage/IndexedDB or Android app storage).
- When you use an AI feature, your prompt is sent **directly from your device** to the AI provider that powers the feature (see section 3).
- We do not collect, sell, share, or track anything. There are no analytics and no ads in the app.

## 2. Data stored on your device

| Data | Where | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Chats & conversations | Device storage | Your chat history |
| Projects & files | Device storage | Your saved projects |
| Image/video history | Device storage | Your generated media list |
| Settings & theme | Device storage | Your preferences |
| API keys & GitHub token (optional) | Device storage | Sent only to the provider they belong to |
| Local account (optional) | Device storage | Login convenience — passwords are hashed |

You can export or delete ALL of this at any time in **Settings → Data** (Export backup / Delete all data), or by uninstalling the app.

## 3. Data processed by third-party AI services (only when you use them)

- **Chat, code generation, auto-fix:** sent directly to the AI engine you select — by default the free keyless engine (Pollinations.ai), or your own API key provider (OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic, Groq, OpenRouter, DeepSeek, xAI, Mistral). These providers process your prompt under their own privacy policies.
- **Image generation:** your image prompt is sent to the free image engine (Pollinations.ai).
- **Voice narration:** uses the built-in speech engine on your device — no network needed.
- **Video generation:** happens entirely on your device.
- **GitHub features (only if you connect a token):** your token and the API calls go directly from your device to api.github.com.

Internet permission is used solely for these AI/API requests.

## 4. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and offers no age-gated content; it is a general productivity tool.

## 5. Your rights (GDPR/CCPA)

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete or export — everything is already yours, on your own device, and fully exportable/deletable in Settings.

Changes to this policy will be published on this page with an updated date.
